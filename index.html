The hook · Honors thesis

Shape-Memory Kinetic Systems

A 3D-printing method that embeds nichrome heaters inside shape-memory polymer — turning printed parts into electrically driven actuators, with wire spacing set by simulation. [Impact metric: e.g. “cut peak ΔT across the part by X%”]

01Hero video — Nepal ↔ NYC sculpture actuating

The context

RoleSole researcher & designer
TeamIndividual honors thesis
Timeline30 weeks
ToolsFusion 360 · Bambu Studio · Laser cutter · Arduino

Problem & constraints

  • Uniform heating: uneven temperature across the SMP causes uncontrolled, partial deformation.
  • In-print embedding: nichrome wire has to be placed precisely and repeatably while the part is printing.
  • Multi-material build: PLA and TPU must print together and still deliver a one-way shape-memory response.

Team4 engineers
Timeline[One semester]
ToolsCNC mill · lathe · waterjet · 3D printing · laser cutter

Problem & constraints

  • Competing goals: acceleration vs. braking vs. controllability vs. manufacturability.
